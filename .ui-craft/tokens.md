# Token Spine

## Primitive
- Canvas: system grouped background
- Surface: system secondary background
- Accent: orange
- Risk: red
- Success: green
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24
- Radius: 10 for compact panels, 14 for the primary cash panel

## Semantic
- Primary focus: orange-tinted surface and orange action icon
- Supporting metrics: neutral surface and tabular numerals
- Risk signal: orange text/surface, red only for a negative forecast amount
- Status: a small dot plus readable text, never color alone

## Component
- Hero cash panel: 20-point inset, 14-point radius, 38-point tabular amount
- Priority row: 8-point vertical spacing, score aligned in a fixed 28-point column
- AI proposal: orange-tinted panel with separate confirm and cancel controls

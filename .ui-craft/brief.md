# Design Brief

## 1. Product purpose
Provides one founder with a private, voice-first operating desk for understanding cash risk, choosing the next important work, and approving AI-proposed plans.

## 2. Primary user
A solo Chinese-speaking founder reviewing the app on an iPhone in short, repeated decision sessions during the working day.

## 3. Principles
1. Wrong is worse than late. AI proposals never write operational or financial data until explicitly confirmed.
2. Cash first, then action. The operating state should reveal liquidity risk before secondary work signals.
3. Three priorities beat an anxious backlog. The command center presents a bounded, ranked daily focus rather than an equal-weight task list.
4. A project must expose its reasoning. Objective, success criterion, hypothesis, risk, blocker, and next action belong together.
5. The data is the interface. Chrome and color recede; orange is reserved for attention and confirmation.

## 4. Success metric
Within 30 seconds, the founder can identify the most urgent cash or execution risk and name the three next actions. A voice request can become a reviewed plan without manual form entry.

## 5. Out of scope
- Multi-user collaboration or approvals
- Bank and payment-provider synchronization
- Autonomous execution of financial or operational writes
- Full desktop workflow editor

## 6. Learned constraints
- 2026-08-23: Use a dense, calm operating view with one orange attention accent. The user needs executive clarity, not a generic task manager.
- 2026-08-23: Voice is the preferred capture path; typing remains an accessible fallback.
